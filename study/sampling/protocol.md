# Empirical Study Sampling Protocol: Bangladesh Application Ecosystem

## 1. Study Population & Inclusion Criteria
This empirical investigation evaluates the prevalence of regulatory-relevant security weaknesses and privacy non-conformance across Android applications distributed for use in Bangladesh.

### Inclusion Criteria:
1. **Platform**: Native Android Application Packages (APK) / App Bundles (AAB).
2. **Jurisdictional Relevance**:
   - Explicitly designed for the Bangladeshi market (Bangla language support, .bd domain endpoints, or registered Bangladeshi corporate entity), OR
   - Listed among the top downloaded applications on the Google Play Store (Bangladesh storefront) in relevant sectoral categories.
3. **Sectoral Stratification**:
   - Applications are stratified into 8 target economic sectors:
     - **MFS (Mobile Financial Services)**: Target $N = 50$
     - **Banking & FinTech**: Target $N = 100$
     - **Government & Public Services (e-Gov)**: Target $N = 100$
     - **E-Commerce & Digital Marketplaces**: Target $N = 100$
     - **Healthcare & Telemedicine**: Target $N = 75$
     - **Transportation & Logistics**: Target $N = 50$
     - **Telecommunications & Utilities**: Target $N = 50$
     - **Education & EdTech**: Target $N = 50$
     - *Total Target*: $N = 575	ext{--}1,000$ applications.

### Exclusion Criteria:
1. Duplicate packages or multiple builds of the same version.
2. Malformed, incomplete, or corrupted APK archives that fail basic zip inflation.
3. Applications requiring physical enterprise hardware or private corporate MDM enrollment.
