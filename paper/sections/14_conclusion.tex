\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We introduced \textbf{Reg2App}, a formally specified, provenance-preserving framework mapping statutory requirements into partially observable software properties. Through 7-tuple $M_i$, 2D observability $\Omega(R_i)$, and 5-state epistemic space $\mathcal{S}$, Reg2App bounds compliance analysis to verifiable client-side evidence while isolating unobservable backend obligations. Operationalized technical controls enable continuous baseline supervision for regulators and CI/CD gating for developers. Future work includes extending extraction to iOS, runtime dynamic instrumentation, and longitudinal deployments.
