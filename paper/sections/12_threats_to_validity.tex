\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}
\noindent\textbf{Internal Validity.} \textit{Benchmark Limitations and Baselines:} Synthetic benchmarks verify implementation correctness ($F_1 = 1.000$) rather than generalized capability; we mitigated this via 53 production apps (14 MFS wallets, 25 banks) and manual adjudication of finding precision ($n=50$, $96.0\%$ [$48/50$, $95\%$ Wilson CI: $86.5\%$--$98.9\%$]) and control recall ($n=51$, overall $94.1\%$ [$48/51$, $83.8\%$--$97.9\%$], defect recall $85.0\%$) on production bytecode. Scanners like MobSF/Androguard lack statutory mappings, restricting baselines to micro-benchmarks (§\ref{sec:benchmark:baseline}). \textit{Sampling Frame:} Corpus combines an MFS census ($n=14$), commercial banks ($n=25/61$), and other sectors ($n=1$ healthcare/media, $n=2$ telecom/transport). Shared vendors and budget disparities confound causal attribution: differences cannot be attributed to regulation alone. \textit{Static Analysis Boundaries:} Static Dalvik analysis faces known boundaries in reflection, dynamic loading, and native JNI~\cite{flowdroid, amandroid, octeau_reflection, ernst_static_dynamic}. Delegating cryptography to native \texttt{.so} libraries (MFS-13, MFS-14) yields $\mathbf{InsufficientEvidence}$, depressing $EC$ to flag dynamic testing needs. Runtime properties (e.g., timeouts under BB-CSF §4.1.3.10) are bounded to structural bytecode invariants ($\mathbf{PARTIAL}$). In our human study, comparing A to B reflects compound utility of structured provenance and code diffs, with C adding native technical terms and authentic legal text.

\noindent\textbf{Construct Validity.} \textit{The Interpretive Gap:} Statutory mandates (e.g., PDPA §15) rarely specify Android APIs~\cite{breaux_requirements}. Reg2App evaluates conformance against operationalized baselines from supervisory circulars~\cite{owasp_masvs} (e.g., certificate pinning for BB-CSF §4.1.3.12); findings reflect baseline non-conformance rather than judicial rulings. Our 3-expert panel achieved high agreement (pre-reconciliation raw agreement $88.9\%$, Fleiss' $\kappa = 0.776$, Krippendorff's $\alpha = 0.782$, 100\% Delphi consensus) across 12 mappings. \textit{Metric Gaming:} Obfuscation or native wrapping cannot produce false passes; unresolved paths force $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \bot$, emitting $\mathbf{InsufficientEvidence}$ ($EC < 0.70$), while Provenance Graph $G$ enables AST inspection.

\noindent\textbf{External and Conclusion Validity.} While evaluated on Bangladesh (BB-CSF v1.0, Draft BD-PDPA, BD-CSA 2023), Reg2App's 7-tuple $M_i$ and operator $\Omega(R_i)$ generalize across jurisdictions (Architectural Property~1), including EU GDPR~\cite{gdpr_2016} and PCI-DSS~\cite{pci_dss_v4}. Reg2App targets Android ($>95\%$ market share in Bangladesh~\cite{statcounter_os_2026}); iOS extraction is planned. Because compliance scores violated normality, we used non-parametric tests: Kruskal-Wallis across eight sectors ($H = 30.37, p < 0.001$), Mann-Whitney $U$ between financial and retail cohorts ($U = 156.0, p < 0.001$, Cliff's $\delta = 1.000$), Wilcoxon signed-rank tests in our human study ($N=60, p < 10^{-5}$), and exact McNemar tests for patch success ($n_1=30$), with Bonferroni correction and raw data in \texttt{human\_study\_n60\_raw.csv}.
