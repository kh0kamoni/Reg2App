\texttt{\subsection{Hypothesis Testing: Sectoral Disparity (RQ5)}}
\texttt{We evaluated the hypothesis that regulated financial applications (MFS and Banking) exhibit stronger technical compliance than unregulated sectors. A Mann-Whitney U test on $ESC$ yields a test statistic of $Z = -4.0988$ ($p \textless{} 0.001$), rejecting the null hypothesis. Regulated financial entities enforce strict transport encryption and modern ciphers, whereas commercial e-commerce applications consistently exhibit multiple high-risk findings, including plaintext HTTP communications, unencrypted local auth tokens, and third-party tracker exfiltration.}
