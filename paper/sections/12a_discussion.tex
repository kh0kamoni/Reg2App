\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Automating regulatory verification introduces fundamental shifts across oversight, engineering, and legal informatics. Operationalized controls provide verifiable evidence of sovereign compliance while isolating server-side obligations.

\noindent\textbf{Practical Implications for Stakeholders.} For regulators, automated static verification transforms supervision from episodic questionnaires into continuous, bytecode-grounded oversight. For software organizations, embedding Reg2App into CI/CD pipelines enables automated compliance gating prior to release. For auditors, Evidence Provenance Graph $G$ bridges legal clauses and technical witnesses, establishing transparent audit trails while focusing manual reviews on unobservable backend obligations ($L_i = \mathbf{NONE}$).

\noindent\textbf{Emerging-Economy Context and Usable Compliance.} In emerging non-Anglophone ecosystems, technical English security reports impose severe cognitive friction. Grounding findings in official gazette Bengali text alongside bytecode witnesses improves comprehension and halves remediation time, bridging the disconnect between corporate legal counsel and software engineering teams.
