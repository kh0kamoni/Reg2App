\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
Our research interfaces four domains: mobile program analysis, cryptographic auditing, privacy policy mining, and legal informatics. Table~\ref{tab:comparison} contrasts Reg2App against prominent static analysis and compliance frameworks.

\noindent\textbf{Mobile Program Analysis and Taint Tracking.} FlowDroid~\cite{flowdroid} introduced lifecycle taint tracking in Dalvik bytecode, extended across ICC by IccTA~\cite{iccta}, Amandroid~\cite{amandroid}, and DroidSafe~\cite{droidsafe}. Scanners like MobSF~\cite{mobsf}, Androguard~\cite{androguard}, and TaintDroid~\cite{chortler} audit against OWASP MASVS~\cite{owasp_masvs}. Reg2App builds upon these foundations but departs via the 7-tuple $M_i$ and operator $\Omega(R_i)$, producing traceable evidence networks bounded by epistemic limits rather than heuristic scores.

\noindent\textbf{Cryptographic and Transport Misuse Detection.} Empirical studies demonstrate widespread mobile cryptographic misuse~\cite{crypto_misuse_ccs, fahl_msc, castle_crypto}. While linters like CogniCrypt~\cite{cognicrypt} enforce isolated CrySL rules, Reg2App anchors cryptographic properties directly to sovereign mandates (e.g., BB-CSF §4.1.3.12, §4.1.5.19), evaluating how parameters substantiate compliance under administrative law.

\noindent\textbf{Privacy Policy Mining and Policy-to-Code Alignment.} Prior studies examine mobile behavior and privacy disclosures: Polisis~\cite{polisis} parsed policies via NLP, Slavin et al.~\cite{slavin_icse} detected policy-to-code gaps, MAPS~\cite{appinspect} audited third-party libraries, and PrivacyStreams~\cite{privacystreams} instrumented dataflows. In data protection, Pandit et al.~\cite{gdpr_ont} modeled consent ontologies, and Hamdani et al.~\cite{hamdani_2023} surveyed GDPR verification. While prior work analyzes \textit{bilateral consistency} against privacy policies, Reg2App addresses \textit{unilateral statutory compliance}: verifying bytecode against sovereign acts while using $\mathcal{S}$ to prevent false compliance on backend obligations.

\noindent\textbf{Legal Informatics and Regulation-as-Code.} Translating statutory text into computational representations is central to legal informatics~\cite{breaux_requirements, legalruleml, gordon_legal_kg, rac_survey}, with Catala~\cite{catala} executing tax statutes and Symboleo~\cite{symboleo} modeling contracts. While they compile statutes into software, Reg2App addresses inverse verification: \textit{evaluating whether third-party production binaries conform to statutory obligations}. Its declarative ontology (Architectural Property~1) decouples legal rules from analyzer logic.

\noindent\textbf{Usable Security and Multilingual Developer Tools.} Security failures frequently stem from poor diagnostic interfaces~\cite{sasse_usable_sec, acar_developers, wash_developers}. Reg2App contributes a bilingual framework bridging legal and software engineering workflows in emerging economies.
