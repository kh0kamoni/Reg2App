\texttt{\section{Conclusion}}
\texttt{\label{sec:conclusion}}
\texttt{In this paper, we introduced \textbf{Reg2App}, an evidence-based framework that bridges natural-language cybersecurity regulations and Android bytecode analysis. By formally modeling observability limits, preserving full evidentiary provenance, and evaluating both controlled benchmarks and production applications in Bangladesh, we demonstrated that regulatory compliance can be scientifically assessed without collapsing into unscientific percentage scores. Furthermore, our localized bilingual evaluation highlights the critical role of linguistic accessibility in usable cybersecurity compliance.}
