\texttt{\section{Threats to Validity \\& Related Work}}
\texttt{\label{sec:threats}}

\texttt{\subsection{Threats to Validity}}
\texttt{\textbf{Construct Validity}: Binary analysis cannot observe backend database encryption or external consent records. We mitigate this through our explicit observability taxonomy and the 3-layer claim boundary.}
\texttt{\textbf{Internal Validity}: Potential errors in regulatory interpretations are mitigated via formal JSON schema validation and independent expert agreement ($\text{Fleiss' } \kappa \ge 0.85$).}
\texttt{\textbf{External Validity}: Our dataset reflects Bangladesh's leading mobile ecosystem; portability to other jurisdictions is facilitated by the decoupled Regulation-as-Code schema.}

\texttt{\subsection{Related Work}}
\texttt{Automated mobile security scanning (MobSF~\cite{mobsf}, FlowDroid~\cite{flowdroid}, Androguard~\cite{androguard}) focuses primarily on general vulnerability detection rather than formal statutory mapping. While legal ontology research has investigated GDPR compliance~\cite{gdpr\_ont}, Reg2App is the first framework uniting Android program properties, partial observability theory, and empirical ecosystem evaluation under emerging national cybersecurity mandates.}
